Apple Inc. Q1 2026 Earnings Call Highlights
Tim Cook announced a record revenue of $124.3 billion, up 6% from Q1 2025. iPhone revenue accounted for $69.7 billion, showing strong demand for the iPhone 17 series with edge-AI chips. Services revenue reached an all-time high of $25.1 billion, growing 14% year-over-year. Luca Maestri noted that the gross margin was 45.9%, at the high end of guidance. Apple returned $27 billion to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases. Regarding inventory levels, Apple expects supply chain bottlenecks to subside by late Q3. R&D expenditure rose to $8.2 billion, reflecting intensive investments in Apple Intelligence and spatial computing headsets.
