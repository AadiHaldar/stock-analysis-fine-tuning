Tesla Inc. SEC Form 10-K Summary (FY2025)
Tesla reported total automotive revenues of $82.4 billion, representing a 12% increase year-over-year. The gross margin for automotive operations fell slightly to 18.2% due to price adjustments in key markets like China and Europe. Operating income reached $9.1 billion, driven by reductions in cost of goods sold (COGS) and manufacturing efficiencies. Capital expenditures for gigafactories in Texas and Berlin reached $7.8 billion. Tesla plans to begin production of its next-generation platform in late 2026, aiming to lower vehicle costs below $25,000. Furthermore, the company is increasing research spending in FSD Supercomputing clusters by $1.2 billion.
